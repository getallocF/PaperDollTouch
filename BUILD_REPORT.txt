================================================================================
LAPORAN ARSITEKTUR & PENGUJIAN — MINECRAFT MOBILE CURSOR (v2.0.0)
================================================================================

1. ANALISIS KOMPATIBILITAS & INTEGRASI ARSITEKTUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Target Game & Loader: Minecraft Bedrock 26.60 (arm64-v8a), LeviLauncher Android
  Preloader Native (LiteLDev/preloader-android v0.2.2, C++20, NDK 28.2.13676358).
- Sistem Lifecycle GUI (Bebas Loop Berat per Tick):
  Menggunakan hook vtable RTTI pada `16GradientRenderer`, `18LivePlayerRenderer`,
  `17PaperDollRenderer`, `21InventoryItemRenderer`, dan `13HoverRenderer`.
  Kursor custom otomatis disembunyikan saat kembali ke gameplay (`hud_screen`)
  atau saat mouse fisik (`AINPUT_SOURCE_MOUSE`) terdeteksi.
- Sistem Render Overlay Zero-Shader (`eglSwapBuffers` + `GL_SCISSOR_TEST`):
  Menggambar pointer putih Windows (bitmap tajam 12x19 dengan outline gelap 1px),
  outline Green Slot Hover (`#38E54D`), dan panel `Mobile Cursor Settings` tanpa
  mengubah shader/VBO RenderDragon.
